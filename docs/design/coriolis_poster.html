Elevation

Sections

Five stations through one half. The centre is deliberately empty out to z = ±10.5 m, which is where the kickoff spawns sit — the first thing anyone touches is the vane band, not a wall.

Colours and intensities are the stock arena's measured values — the warm key #ff956e against the cool fill #95d1ff, and the #ff5013 / #0390ff team pair. That split is what makes an Echo map read as one; the plan is simply fewer and better-placed fixtures.

Build notes

  1. Fixed, do not move. Goals at (0, 0.04, ±36.05), disc at the origin, kickoff spawns at z = ±10, respawns in the chambers. The match logic is tied to these.
  2. Author one half. Everything here describes z > 0; the other end is generated by the 180° turn about Y. Both ends then cannot drift apart.
  3. Collision travels with the geometry. Render placement, the physics body and the Embree raycast tree all move together, or the map collides with things you cannot see.
  4. New models need an actor id. This is the one open problem: an added instance needs an entry in CActorDataResource, and growing that keyed table is not solved yet. Until it is, Coriolis has to be built from re-placed stock models rather than new ones.
